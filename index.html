05 — Questions

GRP & FRP, answered

The questions we hear most before a build begins. Anything else, ask us directly.

What is the difference between GRP, FRP and fiberglass?

They describe the same family of material. GRP (glass-reinforced plastic) and FRP (fibre-reinforced plastic) are composites made of glass fibre set in resin, and fiberglass is the everyday name for both. The result is light, strong and corrosion-resistant, which is why it is used for tanks, linings, vehicle body parts and architectural mouldings.

What does Hussain Engineering fabricate?

Industrial FRP/GRP tanks, chemical-resistant resin linings, scrubbers and cooling tower sheeting; composite auto parts such as spoilers, air ducts, shrouds, mudguards, bonnets and trim covers; and custom work including domes, louvers, canopies, aquaponics beds, and mesh and corrugated GRP sheet.

Which industries do you work with?

Marine and fisheries, oil and gas refining, power generation, hospitality, defence and government, automotive, real estate, water supply and utilities, education, amusement and leisure, conservation and aquaculture.

Is the work done in-house?

Yes. Starting from a sample mould, the moulding, fabrication and finishing are all done in-house, for one-off builds and repeat production runs alike.

Where is Hussain Engineering located?

We work from two sites in Karachi, Pakistan: Survey 32, Gali No. 2, near Quba Masjid, Sherpao Colony, Landhi; and F-5, Korangi Fish Harbour, near Chashma Goth.
