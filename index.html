01 — Legacy

Three Decades of First-of-Their-Kind Fabrication

Pioneering composite engineering for Pakistan's marine, energy, fisheries and civil sectors since the late 1980s.

Hussain Engineering didn't enter the GRP industry — it helped build it in Pakistan, one prototype at a time.

What began as a small Karachi fabrication outfit became the country's reference point for fiberglass-reinforced plastic (GRP) and composite work: the first GRP trawler ever built in Pakistan, the first GRP fishing trawler design later exported to Madagascar, and an award-nominated self-propelled unsinkable barge recognised for Design Excellence in Atlanta, Georgia.

Today the same workshop floor that shaped those firsts runs production for refineries, power plants, hotels, fisheries authorities and the armed forces — alongside a steady civil works practice covering everything from bungalows to school buildings. Three decades in, the constant is the same: design it properly, build it to last, and stand behind it in the field.

№ 01
40,000-litre underground GRP fuel tanks — built to international standard for the Ormara naval base, in continuous use for over 30 years.
№ 02

№ 03

№ 04
