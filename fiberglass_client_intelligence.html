PPRA Strategy for Hussain Engineering
How to get on federal radar as a small proprietorship
Register on EPADS — Free vendor registration at epms.ppra.gov.pk. Exempted from annual fee in first year. Enables you to submit e-bids.
Target DISCOs — HESCO and SEPCO (Sindh-based electricity companies) regularly procure FRP electrical enclosures and panels. Closer to Karachi than IESCO.
Set keyword alerts — PPRA does not have email alerts natively; use TenderAlert.pk or PaperPk as a monitoring layer instead.
